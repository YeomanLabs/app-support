What you need

  • A Microsoft Entra ID tenant with Microsoft Intune
  • An Entra admin to register FieldKit once (about 10 minutes). The setup screen in the app walks through it, and can send the steps to your admin.
  • Intune and Entra roles for each engineer that cover what they need to do

Just looking? Tap Try with demo data on the setup screen to explore with sample devices. Nothing connects to Microsoft.

"Access denied"

The message names the permission or role that's missing. After an admin grants a permission to the app registration, open Settings → Permissions and tap Refresh sign-in. Your own roles still apply: for example, reading BitLocker keys needs an Entra role such as Cloud Device Administrator.

Scanning

Point the camera at the label. FieldKit highlights every barcode it sees; tap the serial number. Labels often carry several barcodes, so FieldKit never picks one for you. You can always type the serial instead.

Installing apps

Intune installs apps that are assigned as Required to a group the device or its user is in. FieldKit installs an app by adding the device or user to one of those groups, so the app needs a Required assignment to an assigned (not dynamic) group. One group per app, such as APP-7-Zip-Install, works best.
