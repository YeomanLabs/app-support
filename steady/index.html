Keeping your data safe

Steady stores everything on your phone. To keep a copy, open Settings, tap Export Backup, and save the folder to iCloud Drive. Restore from Backup brings it all back, including photos.

Estimates

Steady's "estimated amount in body" is a model based on the doses you log and the half-life you set. It is not a blood level and not medical advice. Always follow your prescriber's instructions.
