Steady Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

Steady does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your phone

Your shots, vials, weights, progress photos, reminders, and settings are saved only on your device. Photos are kept inside the app and are never added to your photo library.

If you also use the Roast app, Steady shares a few progress numbers with it on your device (your start date, first and latest weight, and next shot date). They never leave your phone.

Backups

Backups are created only when you choose to export one, and they're saved wherever you pick in the Files app. Steady never uploads them.

What Steady doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • No network requests: Steady never sends data anywhere

Deleting Steady deletes everything it stored.
