Roast Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

Roast does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What stays on your phone

Your widget settings, the quotes you write or edit, and your favorites and hidden quotes are saved only on your device. If you use the companion app, the progress numbers it shares with Roast (such as a start date or weight change) are also stored only on your device.

What Roast doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • No network requests: Roast never sends data anywhere

Deleting Roast deletes everything it stored.
